Designer looks for your lights

HomeGlow turns WLED-powered permanent and holiday lights into something that looks professionally designed, with looks for every holiday and shows that loop on the controller by themselves.

Signature looks

Layered effects with breathing accents, sparkle and a light that sweeps across the house. Hundreds of looks for everyday, accents, holidays, occasions, sports teams and flags.

Holiday Autopilot

Pick the holidays you celebrate. Your lights switch to that holiday's looks on their own, then back to your everyday look. Runs on the controllers, even with your phone off.

Game Day

Choose your teams and game nights start in moving team colors at sunset. HomeGlow can ask first, and waits until after the game to switch to night lighting.

Nightly lights

On at sunset, switch to night lighting later, off before sunrise. One schedule for every controller, with your own look or map on each, and smart power relays switched for you.

Custom Maps

Paint your house light by light for night lighting or accents. Recolor every map at once from Now Playing.

Lights that move together

The house leads and the shop, shed and patio follow, so a chase runs across every building in step.

Colors that look right

A two-minute color check tunes orange, gold, purple and pink to your own lights.

Make it yours

Press and hold any pattern to set brightness, speed and size. Build your own looks and share them with a link.

Siri and widgets

"Turn on HomeGlow", "Make HomeGlow brighter", or tap the widget. Power relays switch on first.

Private and local

No account, no cloud, no subscription. HomeGlow talks to your controllers on your home Wi-Fi, and backs up to your own iCloud.

Pro and holiday packs

Pro unlocks the Builder, Custom Maps, sports and flags, Autopilot and whole-house schedules. Holiday packs add signature looks and shows. One-time purchases.

Away from home

A free, step-by-step guide to control your lights while you're away with Tailscale.

Requires a WLED light controller on your home network. Available on the App Store for iPhone.
