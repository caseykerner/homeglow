Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

The short version: HomeGlow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your devices

Your controllers, patterns, favorites, tweaks and schedules are stored on your iPhone and on your own WLED controllers. To let every phone in your home see the same tweaks and saved looks, HomeGlow saves a small settings file on your controllers. This information travels only over your home network and never reaches us.

Location

If you schedule lights around sunrise or sunset, HomeGlow asks for your approximate location once and writes it to your own controllers so they know when the sun sets. Your location is not sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings.

Local network

HomeGlow uses your local network to find and control your lights and, if you set them up, your smart relays. These connections stay inside your home network.

Internet connections

HomeGlow checks GitHub for the latest WLED version so it can tell you when a controller update is available. That request contains no personal information. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

HomeGlow is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: homeglow.app.support@gmail.com.
