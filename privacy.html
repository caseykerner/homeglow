Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version: HomeGlow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. We never receive information about you or your home.

What stays on your devices

Your controllers, patterns, favorites, tweaks, maps, schedules, Holiday Autopilot choices and the holidays and teams you pick are stored on your iPhone and on your own WLED controllers. To let every phone in your home see the same tweaks and saved looks, HomeGlow saves a small settings file on your controllers. This information travels only over your home network and never reaches us.

iCloud backup

HomeGlow backs up its settings (your controllers, schedules, maps and choices) to your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they come back on a new phone. This backup is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it. You can turn it off for HomeGlow in iOS Settings under your name > iCloud.

Location

If you schedule lights around sunrise or sunset, HomeGlow asks for your approximate location and writes it to your own controllers and power relays so they know when the sun sets. Your location is not sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings.

Local network

HomeGlow uses your local network to find and control your lights and, if you set them up, your smart power relays. These connections stay inside your home network.

Notifications

If you allow them, HomeGlow shows reminders (for example, asking if you're watching tonight's game, or to open the app so your schedule stays current). These notifications are created on your iPhone. No push service or server is involved.

Internet connections

HomeGlow makes a few requests that contain no personal information:

  • Game Day: if you use Game Day, HomeGlow downloads a public schedule of upcoming games from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages). The request is the same for everyone. Your teams and answers stay on your iPhone.
  • Controller updates: HomeGlow checks GitHub for the latest WLED version so it can tell you when a controller update is available.

Like any website, GitHub may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) for these requests under GitHub's privacy policy. We don't receive or use them.

Sharing a look

When you share a look, the look's name and settings are placed in the link itself, after the "#" character. Web browsers don't send that part of a link to any server, so the look goes only to the people you send it to.

Siri, widgets and AI design

Siri phrases and the Home Screen widget run on your iPhone. HomeGlow's AI look designer uses Apple's on-device model where available, so your requests are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us.

Remote access

Our optional guide explains how to use Tailscale to reach your lights while away. Tailscale is a separate service with its own privacy policy. HomeGlow does not require it and does not share anything with it.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive no payment or personal details.

Children

HomeGlow is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: homeglow.app.support@gmail.com.
