Control your lights while you're away

HomeGlow talks to your lights over your home Wi-Fi. To reach them from anywhere, use Tailscale, a free, secure app. One device at home stays on as the "doorway," and your iPhone goes through it. HomeGlow then works away from home just like it does at home.

Free for up to 6 people. About 15–20 minutes. Your schedules, Holiday Autopilot and Game Day already run with your phone off, so you only need this to change things while you're out.

Before you start: your home network range

In HomeGlow, open Help → Control your lights when you're away. It shows your range, worked out from your lights, with a Copy button. It usually looks like 192.168.1.0/24. You'll type it in step 2.

1. Make a free Tailscale account

On your iPhone, install Tailscale from the App Store, open it, and sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft.

2. Set up the doorway at home

Pick one device that's always on at home.

Apple TV (easiest for most homes, tvOS 17 or newer)

  1. On the Apple TV, open the App Store, search Tailscale and choose Get.
  2. Open Tailscale, choose Install VPN Configuration, then Allow, then Connect.
  3. Scan the code it shows with your iPhone's camera and sign in to the same account as step 1.
  4. In Tailscale on the Apple TV, choose Subnet Router → Advertise New Route and enter your range.
  5. Keep the Apple TV as your Home hub so it stays connected while asleep: Settings → AirPlay and HomeKit → HomeKit, and make sure it has a room.

Home Assistant

  1. Go to Settings → Add-ons → Add-on Store, search Tailscale and install it.
  2. Start it, open its Web UI, and sign in to the same account as step 1.
  3. It shares your home network by itself. If you changed its settings, make sure "advertise routes" includes your range.

Synology

  1. In DSM, open Package Center, search Tailscale and install it.
  2. Open Tailscale from the main menu and sign in to the same account as step 1.
  3. Turn on Subnet router ("Advertise subnet routes" in some versions) and enter your range. Tailscale's guide for Synology: tailscale.com/kb/1131.

Mac or PC

  1. Install Tailscale from tailscale.com/download and sign in.
  2. Turn on subnet routing for your range using Tailscale's guide: tailscale.com/kb/1019.
  3. Set the computer to never sleep, or remote control stops whenever it does.

3. Approve your home network

Go to login.tailscale.com → Machines. Find your doorway (it shows a "Subnets" tag), choose ⋯ → Edit route settings, check your range, and save.

4. Stop it from signing out

On the same page, choose ⋯ → Disable key expiry next to your doorway. Without this, Tailscale signs the doorway out after a few months and remote control quietly stops.

5. Test it

On your iPhone, turn on Tailscale, then turn off Wi-Fi so you're on cellular. Open HomeGlow: your lights should show as online and respond.

If it doesn't work

  • Lights show offline away from home: make sure Tailscale is on in the iPhone app and that step 3 is approved.
  • Worked, then stopped: the doorway is off, asleep or signed out. For Apple TV, make it your Home hub. Check step 4.
  • Lights powered by a Shelly relay: that's fine. HomeGlow switches the relay through the doorway too.
