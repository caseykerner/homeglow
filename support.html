Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email homeglow.app.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing. Most emails get a reply within two business days.

Common questions

HomeGlow doesn't find my lights

Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the controller, and that HomeGlow is allowed to use the local network (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). You can also add a controller by its IP address.


What lights work with HomeGlow?

Any light strip or permanent light system run by a WLED controller. Controllers that use their own closed apps can often be swapped for a WLED controller.


How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Pro screen or any holiday pack and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be shared with your Family Sharing group.


A show keeps playing after I close the app

That's by design: shows run on the controller itself. Pick another pattern or turn the lights off in HomeGlow to stop it.


Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
